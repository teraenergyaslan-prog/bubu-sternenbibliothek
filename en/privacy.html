Bubu – Star Library (iPhone and iPad)

Privacy

✦ ☾ ✦

Applies to the app “Bubu – Star Library” for iPhone and iPad. Last updated: October 2026.

In short

The app only remembers what you need for reading together: where you stopped, which stories you have already read and on which evening, which ones are in your Favorites, and how sounds, Sleepytime and the bedtime reminder are set. If you like, it also remembers your child’s first name and birthday (day and month only). All of this stays on this device. The app sends nothing to the internet—not from the widget, not through Siri and not for the bedtime reminder. Purchases in the app are handled by Apple; the app never sees any payment details.

What the app stores on the device

  • your reading position in each story (paragraph and start of sentence), so you can continue there;
  • which storybook was opened last and which story was read last;
  • which stories were read to the end—these become the stars in “My Starry Sky”;
  • the day on which a story was first read to the end—this becomes “Our Memory Book” with date and title;
  • which stories are in your Favorites;
  • optionally, your child’s first name—for the dedication (e.g. “Lena’s Star Library”) and the greeting “Good night, Lena”;
  • optionally, your child’s birthday, day and month only, without the year—for a greeting on that day;
  • whether the bedtime reminder is turned on and at what time it comes;
  • whether sounds are turned on or off;
  • the Sleepytime settings: length of the sleep timer, the chosen sound and its volume, and the strength of the night light;
  • whether the whole library has been purchased—just a yes or no; what the App Store reports is what counts;
  • whether the welcome page, the one-time tips and today’s birthday greeting have already been shown;
  • the language you chose for the app (German or English).

Name and birthday are optional. You can change or delete them at any time under “Family & Settings”. This information contains no age, no contact details and no device or advertising identifier. It is not transmitted to us or to anyone else. Search terms are neither stored nor transmitted. If you use a device backup (iCloud Backup or a backup on your computer), this information may be included in it; that backup belongs to you and is not read by us.

Bedtime reminder

  • It is off at first. Only when you turn it on under “Family & Settings” does iOS ask whether the app may show notifications.
  • When it is on, at most one silent notification arrives in the evening, at the time you chose and without sound. iOS schedules and shows it on the device; there is no server and there are no notifications from the internet.
  • If a first name is entered, it appears in the text of the notification. Depending on your iOS settings, the notification may also be visible on the Lock Screen.
  • You can turn the reminder off at any time under “Family & Settings” or in the iOS Settings.

Sleepytime, widget and Siri

  • Sleepytime: the timer, nature sounds, music box and night light run entirely on the device. The sounds are included in the app.
  • “Breathe with Bubu” and the cuddle-time questions “A Little More Cuddle Time”: they are included in the app. The app does not listen and does not measure anything—no microphone, no sensors, no answers.
  • Search: it only searches the stories included in the app.
  • “Story of the Day” widget: it shows the title and introduction of the day’s story from data installed with the app. It does not read reading progress, Favorites or names, and it transmits no data.
  • Siri and Shortcuts: “Today’s Story” and “Open Favorites” only open the app at the right place. The app transmits no data in doing so. Siri’s speech recognition is a service provided by Apple; Apple’s privacy policy applies to it.

Purchases in the app

The app is free. Book 1, “The Star Forest,” is completely free, and every other story has a free preview. A one-time purchase in the app opens the whole library—no subscription.

The purchase is handled by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes your Apple Account, payment details and the purchase under its own responsibility; Apple’s privacy policy applies (apple.com/legal/privacy). The app never sees any payment details. The App Store only tells it whether the whole library has been purchased; iOS verifies the proof of purchase on the device. Only for this—showing the price, buying, checking and restoring the purchase—does iOS connect to the App Store. Ask to Buy and Family Sharing are also managed by Apple; the app receives no information about your family.

Downloading from the App Store

The app is downloaded and installed through Apple’s App Store. We do not receive payment details such as card numbers. For billing and accounting, Apple provides us with aggregated sales figures, as well as aggregated statistics and crash reports from devices whose users have agreed to share analytics data with app developers. This data is collected by Apple, not by the app.

Deleting

The information is deleted when the app is removed from the device. Name and birthday can also be deleted individually under “Family & Settings”. A purchase stays linked to your Apple Account at Apple; after reinstalling, “Restore Purchases” opens the whole library again.

What the app does not do

  • no internet connection of its own and no transfer of data from the device;
  • no advertising, no analytics or advertising services, no advertising identifier, no tracking;
  • no location, no camera, no microphone, no access to contacts, photos or files;
  • no user accounts, no chats, no links to websites;
  • no notifications, except the bedtime reminder if you turn it on yourself;
  • no subscriptions.

Children

The app is meant for adults and children of preschool and primary school age to read together. Parents can optionally enter a child’s first name and birthday; this information stays on the device and is not collected by us. Before a purchase, a long press protects against accidental taps: the purchase button has to be held for about two seconds. After that, Apple asks for confirmation as usual (e.g. Face ID, passcode or Ask to Buy in the family).

Rights and contact

Depending on where you live, the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection and/or the EU General Data Protection Regulation apply. Since the app transmits no data, we hold no data about you or your child from the app.

Controller and contact: see below. We are happy to answer questions about data protection by email.

Complaints can be made to a data protection supervisory authority: in Switzerland to the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC), in the EU to the supervisory authority of your country of residence.
