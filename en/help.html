We’re so glad you read with Bubu. Here are answers to common questions. If something is missing, just write to us.

We answer as soon as we can—usually within a few days.

What is free, and what costs something?

The app is free. Book 1, “The Star Forest,” with five stories is completely free, and you can read the first page of each of the 365 stories. A one-time purchase opens the whole library—no subscription, no ads.

Does the purchase work for the whole family?

Yes. With Apple’s Family Sharing, everyone in your family can use the whole library on their own devices.

New device or reinstalled the app?

In the app, open “Story Calendar” → “Family & Settings” → “Restore Purchases.” Your purchase is linked to your Apple Account.

English or German?

The app follows your iPhone’s or iPad’s language. You can switch between English and German at any time under “Story Calendar” → “Family & Settings” → “Language.” Stories, cuddle-time questions and all texts change right away.

How does the Reading Lock work?

In a story, tap the lock at the top: then only turning pages and “Breathe with Bubu” work—leaving the story doesn’t. To unlock, press and hold the lock for about two seconds. For even more control, use Guided Access in iOS (Settings → Accessibility).

Bedtime reminder

Under “Family & Settings” you can turn on a gentle bedtime reminder—at most one per evening, without sound. It is off at first.

Sleepytime

In a story, tap the moon: there you’ll find a sleep timer, calm nature sounds, a music box and a warm night light.

Privacy

The app collects no data and sends nothing to the internet. Read more in the privacy policy.
